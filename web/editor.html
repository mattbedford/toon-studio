    How it works

    Drag a joint to move it. Each bone becomes a piece of the puppet, shown in its colour. If a leg lights up two colours, move the knee.

    Add joint, then click where the new joint goes. It hangs off the selected joint.

    Arrow keys nudge the selected joint. Delete removes it. Ctrl+Z undoes.

    Pull apart

    Slide to see every piece on its own, with the dot it turns around.

    Fix the cut

    Pen paints into the selected piece. Eraser takes pixels out of every piece. The pieces come back together while you paint.

    Pieces

      How it works

      Click a piece to select it. Each piece turns around its dot, the joint it hangs from. A small circle of the drawing is copied under every joint so nothing shows a gap when it turns.

      If part of the body ended up in an arm, select the body, pick the pen and paint it back.
