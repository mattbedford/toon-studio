# Bundled backgrounds: sources and licences

Everything in this folder is released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication),
so it can be used, changed and redistributed without attribution. Credits are
given anyway because the people who made these deserve it.

Only CC0 work goes in this folder. Anything bought, or under a licence with
conditions, belongs in `data/backgrounds/`, which stays out of git.

Folders starting with an underscore (`_elements/`) hold parts for composing
your own scenes and are not shown in the background picker.

## kenney/ and _elements/kenney/

Background Elements Remastered by Kenney, https://kenney.nl/assets/background-elements-redux
Licence: CC0 1.0 (see `_elements/kenney/LICENSE-kenney.txt`).
The eight full scenes are in `kenney/`; the individual trees, houses, clouds
and so on are in `_elements/kenney/`. Files were renamed, nothing else changed.
