    How it works

    Drag a joint to move it. Each bone grabs the part of the drawing nearest to it, shown in its colour. If a leg lights up two colours, move the knee.

    Add joint, then click where the new joint goes. It hangs off the selected joint.

    Arrow keys nudge the selected joint. Delete removes it. Ctrl+Z undoes.
